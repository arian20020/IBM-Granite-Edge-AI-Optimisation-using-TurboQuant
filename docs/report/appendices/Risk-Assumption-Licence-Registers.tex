\section{Experiment-Derived Known Issues}

The remaining limits include failed or unavailable SYCL and OpenVINO cases,
incomplete device tracing, unfinished estimator and application studies, and
TurboVec build and quality failures. These limits still apply where other
configurations passed.

\section{Assumption Log}

Table~\ref{tab:assumption-log} gives the selected final outcomes and their
evidence limits. Across the full register, nine assumptions are confirmed, one
is rejected for the tested scope and seven remain pending. An independent
OneDrive recovery test restored all 2,708 final and raw evidence files with
matching paths, sizes and SHA-256 hashes, confirming A-001 and A-015. The full
register contains all 17 assumptions.
