# Won't Have / Exclusions and Claim Boundaries

**Baseline:** MoSCoW v1.2 / RTM v1.3  
**Owner:** Arian B  
**Presentation change:** CHG-014 / CR-014 — no scope change  

> The controlled workbook and full RTM contain rationale, mappings, acceptance criteria, verification methods and evidence paths.

| ID | Priority | Release Role | Lifecycle | Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W-01 | Won't | Out of Scope | Excluded | The project will not train or fine-tune a Granite model. |
| W-02 | Won't | Out of Scope | Excluded | The first release will not support every GGUF, OpenVINO or Hugging Face model. |
| W-03 | Won't | Out of Scope | Excluded | The project will not guarantee that a 32B model runs on the target laptop. |
| W-04 | Won't | Out of Scope | Excluded | The project will not guarantee that every model runs on a 4 GB, 8 GB or 16 GB computer. |
| W-05 | Won't | Out of Scope | Excluded | The project will not claim six-times compression, near-zero quality loss or sub-second latency without its own evidence. |
| W-06 | Won't | Out of Scope | Excluded | The core workflow will not depend on cloud inference. |
| W-07 | Won't | Out of Scope | Excluded | The project will not use real patient data or provide medical advice. |
| W-08 | Won't | Out of Scope | Excluded | The interface will not show an unverified experimental option as a normal working option. |
| W-09 | Won't | Out of Scope | Excluded | The project will not build a full replacement for llama.cpp, OpenVINO, LM Studio or Ollama. |
| W-10 | Won't | Out of Scope | Excluded | Full cross-platform delivery is not required for this release. |
| W-11 | Won't | Out of Scope | Excluded | The application will not automatically re-quantise an already quantised GGUF through an unsafe route. |
| W-12 | Won't | Out of Scope | Excluded | A runtime-only TurboQuant setting will not be presented as a new exportable model file. |
| W-13 | Won't | Out of Scope | Excluded | TurboVec will not be described as directly compressing the original knowledge file. |
| W-14 | Won't | Out of Scope | Excluded | The application will not provide unrestricted model downloads from arbitrary or untrusted sources. |
| W-15 | Won't | Out of Scope | Excluded | The first release will not be a full enterprise RAG, document-management or vector-database platform. |
| W-16 | Won't | Out of Scope | Excluded | The prototype will not claim operational clinical, NHS, school or classroom approval. |
| W-17 | Won't | Out of Scope | Excluded | The first release will not provide multi-user server operation or multiple simultaneous model sessions. |
| W-18 | Won't | Out of Scope | Excluded | The first release will not automatically update runtimes/models or collect prompts/documents for analytics. |
