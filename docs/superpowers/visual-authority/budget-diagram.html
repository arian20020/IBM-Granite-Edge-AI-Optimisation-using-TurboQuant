The same diagram, not fitting

Screens 04 and 05. The bar now scales to estimated peak rather than available memory, so the overflow past the safe limit is visible instead of clipped. The over-budget region is hatched red and the footer names the limiting component — which is what tells the user whether a smaller KV cache or smaller weights is the fix.

What changed

Taller track with a real border; a dashed safe-limit rule drawn on the bar with a named flag above it; reserved memory hatched rather than coloured, so it doesn’t read as part of the model; segment labels reduced to numbers with the words moved somewhere they always fit; a footer line restating peak, limit, headroom and evidence grade in words. And the bar rescales when the model overflows, so “does not fit” is shown rather than implied.

My pick

2 for the main screen, 1 inside the “How was this calculated?” disclosure. Brackets answer the user’s question in three words on the surface; the legend gives the component breakdown to anyone who opens the details. Card 3 is not an alternative — it’s the same component in its unfitting state, and I’ll build it either way.
