If two adjacent positions resolve to the same safe configuration, the app says so; it never invents a lower-quality or unsupported option just to make every stop different.

How the five positions resolve

Quality side

  • Maximum quality: highest-quality complete configuration under the PC's safe limit.
  • Quality: next meaningful frontier point with a modest memory/context benefit and minimal additional quality loss.
  • Balanced: the measured knee of the safe quality-memory-performance frontier.

Efficiency side

  • Efficiency: lower-memory frontier point that remains above the preferred quality/performance target.
  • Extreme efficiency: smallest complete configuration still meeting hard acceptable quality, stability, performance and context thresholds.

What should happen to Automatic?

B
