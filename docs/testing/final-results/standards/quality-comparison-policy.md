# Quality comparison policy

Quality evidence records the prompt suite, rubric, scoring version, score, maximum score, criterion, and evidence source where available. A missing score is an unavailable observation, not a zero score.

Quality scores are rankable only when prompt suite, rubric, scoring version, denominator, and aggregation all match. If any of these differ or are unknown, reports must present the evidence side by side with a visible methodology warning rather than a ranked comparison.

Compatible quality summaries still retain their per-prompt and criterion evidence where it exists so reviewers can inspect exclusions, missing responses, and the scoring basis.
