## Purpose

This register records statements currently treated as true for planning, design, implementation or evaluation but which require evidence before they can be relied upon as confirmed project facts.

The assumptions cover the intended Windows 11 x64 and Intel-focused application, IBM Granite, llama.cpp, OpenVINO, TurboQuant, the later TurboVec feasibility investigation, local operation, controlled evaluation, evidence preservation, licensing and use by non-specialist education and healthcare users.

## Approval decision

The register content has been reviewed and approved as the project’s controlled planning-assumption set.

This approval means:

- the assumptions are relevant and proportionate;
- the project may plan work around them while their outcomes remain visible;
- every assumption has an owner, validation method, consequence and review gate.

This approval does **not** mean that every assumption has been proved true. An outcome changes only when the stated check has reviewable evidence.

## Final evidence review

The report-cut-off review on 13 September 2026 used application commit `1d6fdfe1e8054c5ec489ff6ac3547a108ffa312b`, the final experiment release `unified-final-results-2026-09-01-v2`, and the TurboVec production-scale evidence. ## Review and validation rule

An assumption is not confirmed merely because it appears reasonable, has been discussed or has been approved for use in planning.

Each assumption must retain:

- why the project depends on it;
- how it will be checked;
- the exact evidence reviewed;
- the consequence if it is false;
- a clear `Pending`, `Confirmed`, `Rejected` or `Superseded` outcome;
- an owner and review dates.

Where evidence supports only one specific model, runtime, build, device or test environment, the assumption must be confirmed only for that exact scope. A general compatibility or performance claim must not be inferred from a single successful configuration.
