## Purpose

This register records uncertain events or conditions that may affect project scope, schedule, quality, safety, evidence, reproducibility, licensing or technical delivery.

Version 0.2 adds a broad candidate risk inventory drawn from the current project plans, requirements, research, architecture decisions, testing controls and intended implementation scope. The candidate inventory assumes the intended first release includes Windows 11 x64 with an Intel focus, IBM Granite, llama.cpp, OpenVINO, TurboQuant and TurboVec.

The current controlled requirements still need a separate change-controlled update where they do not match that intended OpenVINO and TurboVec scope. Adding the risks here does not by itself change the Project Definition, MoSCoW baseline or RTM.

## Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Candidate | The risk has been identified but has not yet been fully assessed, approved or assigned complete treatment. |
| Open | The risk exists and requires active treatment. |
| Monitoring | Controls are in place and the risk is being observed. |
| Triggered | The risk event has occurred or its trigger condition has been met. |
| Accepted | The residual risk has been consciously accepted with a recorded reason. |
| Closed | The risk is no longer applicable or has been fully resolved. |
| Superseded | A later risk record replaces this one. |

## Entry rule

A risk record is not complete until its cause, probability, impact, exposure, trigger, validation method, mitigation, contingency, owner, residual risk, status, evidence, traceability and review dates are populated or explicitly marked not applicable with justification.

Candidate rows are an identification inventory. They must not be treated as assessed or controlled until the risk review:

1. confirms that the item is a genuine risk rather than an assumption, constraint, issue or licence fact;
2. merges duplicates where one wider risk can cover several closely related events;
3. assigns probability, impact and exposure;
4. defines a practical trigger, validation method, mitigation and contingency;
5. maps the risk to requirements, work packages, tests, decisions and evidence;
6. records the remaining residual risk;
7. changes the status from Candidate only after review.

High risks must not be treated as controlled merely because they appear in this table. Their mitigation and contingency must be reviewable and linked to evidence.

## Scope and consolidation note

The long candidate inventory is deliberately broad so that important concerns are not lost. The first approved baseline should normally contain fewer, stronger risk records. Closely related candidates may be merged into one assessed risk with the smaller concerns recorded as causes, triggers or examples.

No `PD-05`, `EP-007` or `G-M05` completion or validation claim is made by this inventory update.
