## Purpose

This register records the important uncertain events or conditions that may affect delivery, technical integration, safety, evidence, licensing or the final release.

The original identification exercise produced 254 risk records or candidates. That list has now been consolidated into **37 operational risks**. Duplicate, low-value and overly detailed candidates have been merged into the nearest important risk. Fixed boundaries remain in the Constraint Register, beliefs requiring proof remain in the Assumption Register, and licence facts remain in the Licence Register.

The current controlled release roles are aligned as follows:

- **Core:** Windows 11 x64, Intel focus, IBM Granite, upstream llama.cpp and one verified Experimental TurboQuant route;
- **Should Have:** one official OpenVINO route after its integration gate passes;
- **Later investigation:** identify and assess TurboVec, while full application integration remains deferred unless a later approved change reactivates it.

The full risk decision history is preserved in:

- [Risk Consolidation Map](Risk-Consolidation-Map.md);
- [Candidate Risk Backlog](candidates/README.md).

A risk appearing here does not mean that its controls have already been proven effective. Evidence and residual-risk conclusions must be reviewed at the stated gate.

## Rating method

The controlled probability, impact and exposure method is defined in [Control-and-Validation-Plan.md](Control-and-Validation-Plan.md).

| Exposure | Treatment rule |
|---|---|
| Critical | Immediate owner attention; complete trigger, validation, mitigation, contingency and residual-risk review before the dependent gate. |
| High | Active treatment and evidence review before the dependent gate. |
| Medium | Proportionate controls and scheduled monitoring. |
| Low | Monitor and retain the record while relevant. |

## Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Open | The risk exists and requires active treatment. |
| Monitoring | Controls are operating and the risk is being observed. |
| Triggered | The risk event has occurred or its trigger has been met. |
| Accepted | The residual risk has been consciously accepted with a recorded reason. |
| Closed | The risk is no longer applicable or has been resolved. |
| Superseded | A later risk record replaces it. |

## Entry and maintenance rule

A risk record is not complete merely because all table cells contain text. The validation method, mitigation and contingency must be realistic, and the cited evidence must be reviewed before the risk is treated as controlled.

New risks should be added only when they describe a materially different uncertainty. A new symptom, example or failure mode should normally be added to an existing risk’s cause, trigger or evidence rather than creating another duplicate row.
