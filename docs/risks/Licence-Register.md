## Purpose

This register records the licence and permitted-use position for third-party models, runtimes, libraries, forks, datasets, prompt sets, icons, fonts, copied research code, executables and other external material used by or distributed with the project.

No licence decision has been baselined in this file yet. Candidate components will be identified and reviewed before the first controlled baseline is frozen.

## Review-status vocabulary

| Review status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending | The licence or permitted-use position has not been fully reviewed. |
| Approved | Use is permitted for the stated project purpose and packaging decision. |
| Restricted | Use is permitted only under recorded conditions. |
| Rejected | The component or material must not be used or distributed in the proposed way. |
| Superseded | A later version or licence decision replaces this record. |

## Entry rule

Each licence record must identify:

- the exact component, model, dataset, asset or external material;
- its authoritative source and exact version or revision;
- the licence name and reviewable licence evidence;
- whether modification and redistribution are allowed;
- attribution, notice, source-disclosure or other obligations;
- whether the item will be bundled, downloaded separately, referenced only or excluded;
- the reviewer, review status, owner and review dates;
- any unresolved restriction or legal uncertainty.

Public availability or presence on GitHub does not by itself prove that modification, redistribution or packaging is permitted.
