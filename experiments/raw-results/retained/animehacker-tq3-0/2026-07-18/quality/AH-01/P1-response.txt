Here’s a breakdown of how KV cache quantization helps offline models:

*   **Reduced Memory Footprint:** Quantization drastically shrinks the model’s size, crucial for offline use.
*   **Faster Inference:** Smaller models translate to quicker processing, improving performance.
*   **Improved Offline Capability:** Enables running models on devices with limited RAM.
*   **Benefits:**  Significant reduction in required memory, faster response times.
*   **Limitations:**  Potential for slightly reduced accuracy, requires careful tuning.

**Check:**  Before deploying, test the model’s performance on your laptop’s hardware to ensure acceptable speed and accuracy.
