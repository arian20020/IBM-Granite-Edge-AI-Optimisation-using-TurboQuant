BENEFIT: Reduced storage requirements by compressing key-value data, leading to lower disk usage and potentially faster data retrieval due to smaller footprint.

LIMITATION: Compression and decompression processes introduce computational overhead, which may impact performance, especially for high-frequency write operations or in environments with limited processing power.

CHECK: Verify the integrity of compressed data by periodically running a decompression test on a sample subset of the KV-cache to ensure that all data can be accurately reconstructed without loss.
