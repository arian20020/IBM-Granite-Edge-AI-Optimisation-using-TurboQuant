BENEFIT: Reduced storage requirements and improved cache hit rates by compressing key-value pairs, leading to faster data retrieval and lower I/O costs.

LIMITATION: Compression and decompression overhead can introduce latency, especially for high-frequency read operations or when dealing with data that is already highly compressible.

CHECK: Validate the integrity of compressed data by periodically decompressing a sample of the KV-cache and comparing the original and reconstructed key-value pairs to ensure no data loss.
